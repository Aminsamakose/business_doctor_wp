Free Business Health Assessments (SME360, AgriFood360, ESO360) with scoring, printable reports, lead management and versioned question banks.

== Description ==

Business Doctor gives small businesses, agribusinesses and enterprise support organisations a free 25-question Business Health check. Visitors see their overall score, band and six dimension scores straight away, and can print or email their result. Staff follow up each result as a lead, edit the question banks with version control, and see which business areas score weakest.

Add the assessment to any page with the shortcode `[business_doctor_assessment]`, or limit it to one assessment with `platform="SME360"`, `platform="AGRIFOOD360"` or `platform="ESO360"`.

Individual answers are scored on the server and never stored.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin ZIP from Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, then activate it.
2. Open Business Doctor > Settings and set the full-assessment page, privacy page and notification email.
3. Add a Shortcode block with `[business_doctor_assessment]` to a page.
